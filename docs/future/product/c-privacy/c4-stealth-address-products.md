## 1. "Disposable Payment Address Generator" - Receive with New Address Every Time

**Product Description**:
A service where users only need to register one public meta-address, and senders can automatically generate disposable receiving addresses for payments. No need to create new addresses manually.

**User Experience**:
Ha-neul Kim (32, online shopping mall operator) receives cryptocurrency payments from customers. Previously, she used the same address, allowing customers to see her total sales. Now, by registering a stealth meta-address on her shopping mall, a unique disposable address is automatically generated for each customer payment. Customer A's payment and Customer B's payment are not linked to each other, and only Ms. Kim can confirm and receive all payments.

**Observable Benefits**:
- Automatically generates unique payment address for each customer
- Sales information protected as payments cannot be linked together
- Privacy secured without the hassle of address management

## 2. "Freelancer Anonymous Invoice" - Client-Separated Payment Service

**Product Description**:
A service that provides freelancers with different payment addresses for each client, ensuring clients cannot know about each other's existence or payment amounts.

**User Experience**:
Jun-ho Lee (29, freelance developer) is working on projects for 5 companies simultaneously. He sends stealth address-based invoices to each company. The address Company A pays to is completely different from Company B's payment address, and no company can know how much Mr. Lee receives from other clients. Mr. Lee can maintain negotiating power while dealing independently with each client.

**Observable Benefits**:
- Maintains negotiating power through information isolation between clients
- Total income scale not exposed to individual clients
- Ensures confidentiality of business relationships

## 3. "Sponsor Appreciation System" - Anonymous Sponsorship Receipt and Acknowledgment Display

**Product Description**:
A service that allows creators to provide unique addresses to each sponsor when receiving sponsorships, hiding sponsor identities while delivering thank-you messages.

**User Experience**:
So-yeon Park (26, YouTuber) receives cryptocurrency sponsorships from fans. Using the stealth address system, each sponsorship comes to a unique address, and only Ms. Park can confirm who sponsored. Fans cannot see each other's sponsorship amounts, allowing them to support purely without comparison or competition. Ms. Park can send personal thank-you messages while respecting sponsors' privacy.

**Observable Benefits**:
- Healthy sponsorship culture formed as sponsors cannot compare amounts
- Only creator can verify sponsor information
- Sponsors' financial situations not exposed to other fans
